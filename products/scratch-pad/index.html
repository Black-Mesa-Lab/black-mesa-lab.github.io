Scratch Pad

Say it to Siri; get a planned day.

Scratch Pad is a kitty-themed iOS app for capturing ideas the moment you have them. Say “Hey Siri, add to Scratch Pad” and dictate; Scratch Pad turns it into a task with a priority and a suggested time, and can plan your day against your real calendar.

  • Hands-free capture with Siri and App Intents.
  • “Plan my day” and “what’s next” against your own calendar.
  • Works with your own Google account for Calendar and Tasks.

Planned for the App Store.
