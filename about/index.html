About Black Mesa Labs

Black Mesa Labs is an independent software studio, operated by Black Mesa Labs LLC. We design, build and publish native software for macOS and iOS, and we are the publisher of record for our apps on Apple's platforms.

We work in small, end-to-end steps: one thin, real path through a product first, proven in use, then thickened. Most of the code is written with AI agents under human direction, inside guardrails we take seriously: permission fencing, independent verification, and a human decision for anything that cannot be undone.
